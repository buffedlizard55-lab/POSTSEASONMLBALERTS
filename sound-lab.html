What the site is playing right now

One alert sound for the whole page: a cash-register cha-ching. It prefers a real recording installed under assets/audio/ and falls back to a synthesized cha-ching when there is none, so a fresh clone is never silent.

“Play the alert as the site plays it” goes through the real code path (ReplayFeed.playAlertSound()), so it obeys the same 2.5 s cooldown that stops overlapping alerts when several games report at once. The two “only” buttons bypass the cooldown so you can A/B them back to back.

Where to get one you are allowed to ship

The video this request came from — YouTube trR5YxZjfes, channel cashregistersound — links in its own description to a paid Bandcamp release. It is not free to copy, and nothing here is ripped from it. These two were checked page by page:

  • Cash Register (Kaching) — Sound Effect, Modestas123123, 0:03, Pixabay Content Licence. The closest freely hosted match to the reference video — same class of stock SFX, same title pattern.
  • Cash Register Fake, freesound_community / CapsLok, 0:02, Pixabay Content Licence — and the underlying Freesound 184438 is CC0, so this is the more conservatively licensed of the two.

Pixabay Content Licence (summary): free for commercial use, no attribution required, may not be sold or redistributed standalone. No account needed to download a sound effect. Whichever you use, record it in assets/audio/LICENCE.md before committing — see assets/audio/README.md.

Neither is the video's sound. Nothing freely hosted is; the exact recording has to be bought from that Bandcamp release, and then its own terms decide whether it can ship in a public repository.
